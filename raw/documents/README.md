# raw/documents

Files a person handed you. Decks, PDFs, contracts, briefs, one-pagers, filled-in forms.

**The test:** a human authored it and gave it to you as a finished artifact. Their onboarding form, their pitch deck, a signed statement of work, a brief from a client, a strategy doc from a partner.

Name files `YYYY-MM-DD-who-what.pdf`, for example `2026-03-01-example-client-onboarding-form.pdf`. Keep the original format. Converting a deck to markdown loses layout that sometimes carries the meaning, and the wiki page you write from it is where the readable version belongs.

**Not here:** something you wrote goes in `../../company-wiki/`. Something published on the open web goes in `../external-sources/`. A machine-generated report goes in `../data-exports/`.

Contracts and anything with commercial terms deserve a second look before committing. If it should not be readable by everyone who clones this repo, it does not belong in the repo at all.
