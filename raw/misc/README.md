# raw/misc

The landing zone. Anything that does not fit another bucket goes here, and that is the entire rule.

**The test:** you hesitated. If deciding where something goes takes more than a second, it goes here and the decision happens at triage instead.

This bucket exists so that "do not create a new top-level folder" is a rule people can actually follow on a bad day. Without a guaranteed destination that rule gets broken the first busy week, and after that the top level grows forever. Every store that stayed tidy had one of these, and it was messy on purpose.

## How it stays useful

Triage on a cadence, not at drop time. Once a week or once a fortnight, take the whole bucket and sort it in one pass:

```
List everything in raw/misc/ with a one-line summary of what it is.
For each item tell me: which bucket it belongs in, whether it is worth
a wiki page, or whether it should be deleted. Do not move anything yet.
```

Then approve the list and let the agent move the files.

An item that survives three triage passes untouched is telling you something. Delete it, or write the page it has been waiting for.

Do not tidy this folder out of existence when it is finally empty. Empty is the healthy state between triage passes, not evidence that the bucket was unnecessary.
