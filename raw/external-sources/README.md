# raw/external-sources

Material from outside your company. Saved articles, posts, competitor pages, benchmark reports, teardowns, reference examples worth copying from.

**The test:** you did not write it and nobody sent it to you directly. If it came off the public web or out of somebody else's feed, it belongs here.

Name files `YYYY-MM-DD-source-topic.md`, for example `2026-07-19-vendor-blog-outbound-benchmarks.md`. Keep the URL and the capture date in the first two lines of every file, because half of what you save here will be edited or taken down within a year and the claim you built a page on needs a traceable origin.

**Not here:** a report a vendor emailed you is a document, so `../documents/`. A competitor's pricing you scraped into a spreadsheet is machine output, so `../data-exports/`.

Nothing here is a citable source until a human distills it into a wiki page. Somebody else's benchmark is a claim you have not checked, and the distillation step is where it either gets checked or gets labelled as unverified.
